From the very start of the first lockdown, I worked for two years alongside the Madame Arthur cabaret, with Jacques Safarian, devising a whole variety of video formats to keep the venue alive despite its closure: music videos, recordings, short formats, all ways of keeping the audience connected to the cabaret’s world until its doors reopened.
