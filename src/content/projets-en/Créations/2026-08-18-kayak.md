Another entry in the 48 Hour Film Project, this time with the crime thriller drawn as the mandatory genre. With David Rinaldi, we turned the genre on its head: here, the murderer files a complaint with the police… because his victim ran away. A black comedy that twists the conventions of the crime thriller to the point of absurdity.
