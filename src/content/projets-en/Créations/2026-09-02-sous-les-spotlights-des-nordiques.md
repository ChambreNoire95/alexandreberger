For Blønd and Blönd and Blónd, a French-Swedish trio reinventing French chanson with absurd, offbeat humour, I co-created and edited “Sous les Spotlights des Nørdiques” (Under the Nørdiques’ Spotlights), directed by Iván González: the pilot of a completely crazy show, featuring singer Oldelaf.
