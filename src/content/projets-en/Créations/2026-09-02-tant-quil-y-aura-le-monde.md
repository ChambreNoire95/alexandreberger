In late 2025, I wanted to pay tribute to the firefighters of Val d’Oise through a fiction inspired by real life, built from footage shot over the months in the field. A gesture to highlight the beauty of the commitment of these women and men who risk their lives every day to rescue the public. Made for the Nikon Film Festival.
