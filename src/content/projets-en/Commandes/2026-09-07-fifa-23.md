Omar Da Fonseca and Benjamin Da Silva, the cult commentary duo of Liga on BeIN Sports, become the new French voices of FIFA 23. Known for their energy and humour, they lend their voices to all the main game modes after almost a year of recording. For the game’s release, they face off in front of the PlayStation and give their predictions for the 2022 World Cup.
