Built around the Disney+ series “Parallèles”, this case study presents a media innovation campaign centred on an interactive bot that extends the world of the series. Honoured with the 2023 Grand Prix Stratégies de l’Innovation Média (Gold), it won in the categories Best Communication Campaign, Best Event, Special Operation and Influence.
