For “The King Remains”, an event organised by Team Vitality on 12 March 2024 on the Trocadéro esplanade, I directed the official aftermovie. In front of the Eiffel Tower, Mathieu “ZywOo” Herbaut announces the extension of his contract until 2026, seated on a golden throne of weapons before the Counter-Strike fan community gathered for the occasion. The film captures the excitement of the Paris crowd and the spectacular scale of this esports announcement in the heart of Paris.
