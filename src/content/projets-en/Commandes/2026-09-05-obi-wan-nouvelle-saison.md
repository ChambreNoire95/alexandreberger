For the release of the new season of Obi-Wan Kenobi on Disney+, the agency Trafic devised an activation bringing together Henry Tran, formerly of the duo Le Rire Jaune, and Deujna, a content creator followed by over a million subscribers: the two influencers are first initiated into lightsaber combat, then face off in a duel filmed on Red Dragon cameras.
