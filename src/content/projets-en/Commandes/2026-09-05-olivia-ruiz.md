As part of the “OFF du Bon Marché Rive Gauche” series, shot with the store closed, Olivia Ruiz came to present her first novel, “La commode aux tiroirs de couleurs” (The Chest of Coloured Drawers), one of the year’s big literary successes. Made during the second lockdown, this literary conversation hosted by journalist Kerenn Elkaïm was captured with multiple cameras and streamed live on social media in a 45-minute live show. Accompanied by her guitarist, Olivia Ruiz closed the discussion by performing a few songs live.
