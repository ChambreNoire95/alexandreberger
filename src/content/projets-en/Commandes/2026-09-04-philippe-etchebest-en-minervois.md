For the 30th anniversary of the Minervois AOC (created in 1985), the Syndicat Cru Minervois came up with a multi-episode road movie across the region, between Narbonne and Carcassonne: “Mon Minervois, 30 ans d’aventures” (My Minervois, 30 Years of Adventures). Chef Philippe Etchebest takes part in one episode, meeting winemakers along the way in a land of creativity.
