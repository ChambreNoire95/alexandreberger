Directed by Mathieu Spadaro for L’Équipe Explore, this documentary is devoted to the 2022 Ballon d’Or and the race for eternity it represents for the greatest players. I worked on it as camera operator, in charge of the interviews with Zinédine Zidane, Thibaut Courtois and Ada Hegerberg, as well as additional shots such as the visit to the Lyon academy.
