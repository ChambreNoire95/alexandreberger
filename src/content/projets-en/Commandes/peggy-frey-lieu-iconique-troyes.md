A fashion journalist turned influential Instagram figure (@peggfrey), Peggy Frey steps into the historic Petit Bateau factory in Troyes, cradle of the brand founded in 1893. From the knitting workshop to the sewing room, she discovers the manufacturing stages of its iconic pieces and the French textile know-how that has endured for more than a century.
