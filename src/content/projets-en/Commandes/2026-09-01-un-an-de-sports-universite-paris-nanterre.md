For a whole academic year, I followed sport as it is practised at Paris Nanterre University. From the swimming pool to the rugby pitch, from the gymnastics apparatus to the rhythmic gymnastics floor, I went to meet the students where they train.

This long-term project resulted in a film and a photo exhibition, made for the Trophées Sportifs, the sports awards given by the University.

Here is a selection of photographs from this year spent alongside them.
