The film goes behind the scenes of Destination Vegas, a project Johnny Hallyday dreamed up in 1996: to offer 5,000 French fans a trip to Las Vegas to experience a one-off concert at the Aladdin Theatre, a landmark of American rock’n’roll. Between preparations, the crossing of the Atlantic and the build-up of adrenaline before the show, this documentary follows the backstage of an unusual gamble: bringing together the myth of the King and the fervour of his most loyal audience.
