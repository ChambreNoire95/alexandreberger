Founded in 1852 by Aristide and Marguerite Boucicaut, Le Bon Marché Rive Gauche is the world’s first department store still in business. In this series hosted by Stéphane Bern, the visionary couple behind the place is retraced through their story and their innovations, which kept staying ahead of what customers expected in the late 19th century.
