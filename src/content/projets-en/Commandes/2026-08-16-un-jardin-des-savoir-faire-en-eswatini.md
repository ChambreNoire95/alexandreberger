Cyrille Varet, president of Dessine l’Espoir, presents the Garden of Know-How, set up in Eswatini in 2021 by the association and its local partner Fundzisa Live, as part of the Porte-Bonheurs (Lucky Charms) operation with the support of Le Bon Marché Rive Gauche. On a one-hectare plot, the agroforestry garden hosts a training centre for sustainable farming techniques, an organic seed greenhouse, a sewing and crafts workshop, and a kitchen dedicated to processing vegetables and raising awareness of nutrition.
