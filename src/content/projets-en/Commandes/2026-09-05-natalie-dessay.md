On the occasion of the “Porte-Bonheurs” (Lucky Charms) exhibition and alongside the work of the Dessine l’Espoir association, soprano Natalie Dessay performs a digital concert at Le Bon Marché Rive Gauche, accompanied by her pianist Philippe Cassard, with whom she has given recitals on the greatest stages since 2012.
