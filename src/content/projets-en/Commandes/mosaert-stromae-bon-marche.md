As director of most of Le Bon Marché Rive Gauche’s cultural films during its artistic direction from 2018 to 2022, I followed the building of this project step by step, from the first sketches to the opening night, to make the film shown in store that traces the whole story of this “Fantastic Factory” imagined by Stromae and Mosaert. I also attended the studio photo shoot for the visuals of the event held in Brussels, as well as the dance rehearsals with choreographer Marion Motin.
