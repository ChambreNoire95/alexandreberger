Backed by Agropolis Fondation, the One Planet Fellowship supports African researchers who specialise in climate and biodiversity issues, connecting them with European scientists through mentoring and exchanges. I directed all three parts of a documentary series about the programme: a first film shot in Italy alongside two fellows mid-exchange with their mentors, a second in Montpellier during Science Week, a highlight of science popularisation, and a third in Nairobi for the graduation ceremony, the culmination of the researchers' journey.
